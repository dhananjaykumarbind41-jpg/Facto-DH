Why is the sky blue?

Sunlight contains many colours, but the atmosphere scatters blue light more strongly.

This scattering is called Rayleigh scattering. It makes the sky appear blue during the day.

Largest planet

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System.

Jupiter is a gas giant and has many moons.

Why do we yawn?

Yawning is a natural body action linked with tiredness and changes in alertness.

Scientists continue to study all the reasons why humans yawn.

Plants make food

Green plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food.

This process is called photosynthesis.

Home of the world's tallest mountain

Mount Everest is in the Himalayas, on the Nepal–China border.

Its height is about 8,849 metres above sea level.

Save water

Small actions, like turning off the tap while brushing, can reduce water waste.

Saving water helps people, farms and ecosystems.
